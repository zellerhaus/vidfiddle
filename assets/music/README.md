# Starter music

One track per mood, all released into the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You can use them in published videos
without attribution, though credit is appreciated.

| Mood      | Track                                    | Artist     | Source                                                                                     |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| upbeat    | Feeling Human Again                      | HoliznaCC0 | [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/spring-woke-me-up)       |
| calm      | Roof Tops                                | HoliznaCC0 | [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/summer-air-lo-fi)        |
| inspiring | Spring On The Horizon                    | HoliznaCC0 | [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/background-music)        |
| dramatic  | Determined Pursuit (epic orchestra loop) | Emma_MA    | [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/determined-pursuit-epic-orchestra-loop)       |
| playful   | A Small Town On Pluto (Music Box)        | HoliznaCC0 | [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/background-music)        |

The files are trimmed to their first 90 seconds with a short fade-out. Determined Pursuit is kept
whole because it is built to loop. All are leveled to -16 LUFS and encoded as 128 kbps MP3.

Add your own tracks to `music/` rather than here (see [music/README.md](../../music/README.md)).
